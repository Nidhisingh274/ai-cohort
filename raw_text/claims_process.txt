Claims Process Guide
This document explains how to file a claim.
Step 1: Submit Your Claim
Claims must be submitted within 90 days of the service date. Include your member ID and procedure details.
Step 2: Claim Review
Our team reviews claims within 10 business days. You will receive a status update by email.
Step 3: Reimbursement
Approved claims are reimbursed within 15 business days to your registered payment method.
